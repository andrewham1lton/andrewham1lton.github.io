Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email mildvandalism@protonmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Every picture keeps its own progress, colors, undo history and position. There is no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open the help screen (the question mark) and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I save a finished picture?

On the finished picture, tap Share, then Save Image.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where your region requires it, tap Privacy choices in the help screen.
