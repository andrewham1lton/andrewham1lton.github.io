Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Mild Vandalism is a coloring app for iPhone made by Andrew Hamilton. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Your pictures stay on your phone

Your coloring progress, finished pictures, badges and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, and the developer never receives your pictures. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Usage and diagnostics

To find bugs and make the app better, the app sends the developer anonymous usage and diagnostic information: which pictures are opened and how far they get, whether ads load and show, Remove Ads taps and results, settings changes, errors, launch time, and Apple's crash, hang and performance reports for the app. Each report includes the app version, iOS version, device model and language. Our server notes your approximate country and does not keep your IP address.

The app links these reports with a random install number it creates itself, so it can tell whether people come back. It is not your advertising identifier, your name or your Apple Account, and the developer cannot use it to find out who you are. In the UK and Switzerland the app sends no install number. Deleting the app resets it.

This information is stored on Cloudflare, the developer's hosting provider. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used to track you. Detailed reports are deleted after 90 days (Apple's diagnostic reports after 180 days).

Ads

The app shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob after some finished pictures. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

The app does not ask for permission to track you, so it does not share your device's advertising identifier with Google.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google's consent form lets you choose how your information is used for ads. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices in the app's help screen.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, Apple processes the payment. The developer never sees your payment details; the app only learns that the purchase was made.

Photos

When you choose Save Image, the app asks for permission to add the picture to your photo library. It can only add pictures. It cannot see your photos.

Children

Mild Vandalism is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
